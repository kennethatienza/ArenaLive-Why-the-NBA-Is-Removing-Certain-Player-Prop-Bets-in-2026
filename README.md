## Why First-Play Props Drew Fresh NBA Scrutiny

On **October 16, 2026**, the league sent **new bet rules** to its sports book partners. The bans began with the **2026–27 NBA season**, and they cover only some types of bets. The aim is **to cut bets where one short play or early exit could sway results**.

A **player prop** is a bet on one player's stats, not the whole final score. Common props cover **points, boards, and assists** across a game, while some track one play. **The league drew a line between those types**, rather than ban all bets on player stats.

The NBA asked **partner books** to stop bets it sees as easy to abuse or fix. That request does not force **every site with live scores** to post or remove such markets. A scores page can still show **game facts** without taking bets or joining a league deal.

## Opening Tip And First Stat Bets Leave Menus

The bans cover **opening tip results** and who makes the first key play of a game. The list includes **first baskets, threes, boards, assists, steals, and blocks** by a named player. Such bets hinge on **one brief play**, not how a team does over the full game.

Bets that a player will **miss a set shot** are also on the list of bans. This can mean **a first shot, next shot, or one miss within a set time**. **One missed shot is not proof of cheating**, even when books can no longer sell that bet.

## Under Bets Face Two Distinct New Limits

The league bars **unders on players who average or are expected to play under 12 minutes**. The rule uses **usual or planned time on court**, not the minutes logged once play ends. **A short stint means fewer chances to earn stats**, which makes some bets easier to sway.

Its partner books must also stop **parlays with more than one under on the same player**. One banned slip might pair **under points with under boards** for that player in one bet. **That combo is barred**, but a plain under line on its own may still be sold.

Reports also note bans on **alternate under lines** that offer large pay for a rare result. These can set **a very low stat mark** far from the main line shown to fans. **Not all under bets face a ban**, so a claim that every points under vanished is false.

## Porter And Rozier Cases Explain The Shift

The **NBA barred Jontay Porter for life in 2024** after its own probe into bet fraud. His case raised fears about **inside tips and weak stat totals** used to shape some wagers. **The league ban is a fact**, but a poor night on court does not show fraud.

**US prosecutors charged Terry Rozier** over claims tied to advance news about his time on court. **The charges remain claims, not a court verdict**, and they should not be treated as proof. Both cases help show **why short stints and linked under bets** now face far more checks.

## Bet Watch Tools And Staff Training Add Safeguards

The NBA also set out **a new in-house tool** to flag odd bets linked to games. News reports say it checks **injury news, web posts, and bet trends** when it spots risks. **A software flag means review is needed**, not that a player has fixed the game.

The league says **players, coaches, and team staff** must attend in-person talks on betting rules. **Charles Tillman, a former NFL player and FBI agent**, will lead those live training sessions. Such talks can show **why private team news must stay private**, even when games end.

The NBA also plans **new help for players who face threats** after fans lose their bets. Its steps include **ways to report threats and curb online abuse**, not just check wagers. **No lost bet gives anyone a right to abuse or threaten an athlete**, anywhere online.

## Philippine Fans Should Know Where These Bans Apply

For **Filipino NBA fans aged 21 and up**, some partner books may now list fewer props. Yet **NBA partner rules are not Philippine law**, nor do they give local books approval. **A missing prop does not prove a site's license**, or show that a game was fixed.

Bet lists can change **by place, book, law, or game date**, even for one NBA match. Reports say the league's new limits will also cover **WNBA games from 2027** under partner deals. **Prediction markets were outside this memo**, so it did not set a ban for every venue.
